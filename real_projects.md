01 --- Commerce & Business Management Platform

Tags: E-commerce · Business Platform · Analytics

Overview

A customized commerce and business management platform built to connect
the customer shopping experience with the operational systems behind a
growing direct-to-consumer business.

The platform brings commerce operations, marketing, customer activity,
inventory, financial visibility, subscriptions, and business analytics
into a centralized administration experience.

The Challenge

A growing commerce business needed more than a standard storefront.
Day-to-day operations, marketing activity, customer behavior, financial
performance, inventory, and order activity needed to be understood
through one connected system.

The platform also needed to support detailed business analysis rather
than relying only on headline revenue figures.

Our Approach

We built a customized e-commerce experience together with a
comprehensive administration platform designed around the business's
operational workflow.

The system was structured to give the business team a central place to
manage commerce operations and understand performance across multiple
areas of the business.

Key Capabilities

Custom e-commerce storefront

Product, category, and inventory management

Order and fulfilment operations

Customer management

Returns, refunds, and RTO tracking

Marketing campaigns and promotional workflows

Coupon, referral, and affiliate management

Email campaign management

Subscription management

Sales and product analytics

Marketing and channel performance analytics

Customer retention and segmentation analysis

Financial and profitability analytics

Settlement and tax-related reporting

Content, blog, review, and SEO management

Role-based administration

Operational alerts and reporting

Business Intelligence

The administration platform was designed to move beyond basic revenue
reporting.

Its financial and analytical model covers areas such as:

Revenue and order performance

Cost and margin visibility

Customer acquisition and lifetime value

Returns and refund impact

Inventory and batch/expiry information

Channel attribution

Subscription and retention metrics

Product-level profitability

Trend and variance analysis

Break-even analysis

Business health indicators

Automated strengths, weaknesses, and watchlist insights

The underlying specification specifically emphasizes unit economics,
retention, cash health, target-versus-actual analysis, profitability,
and decision-oriented diagnostics rather than reporting revenue alone.

Outcome

The result is a unified business platform where the storefront and the
operational side of the business work together, giving the team a
broader view of commerce performance and the information needed to make
informed decisions.

Privacy

Client identity, private business data, customer information, internal
architecture, infrastructure configuration, and proprietary
implementation details are intentionally not included.

03 --- Financial Operations & Profitability Platform

Tags: Business Software · Financial Operations · Analytics

Overview

A custom web and mobile business management platform built for a
financial-services operation that needed a clearer way to understand
client activity, settlements, and overall business performance.

The Challenge

The business owner had limited time to manually review client-level
information and monthly financial activity.

The goal was to make important business information accessible through a
centralized system rather than requiring constant manual review and
calculation.

Our Approach

We built the platform from scratch around the company's specific
financial and operational workflow.

The system collects operational data entered through the management
interface and turns it into consolidated dashboards, client views,
historical information, and reports for the business owner.

The platform includes both a web experience and a mobile experience so
the owner can access business information more conveniently.

Key Capabilities

Client management

Client-wise financial tracking

Monthly settlement visibility

Client history

Business performance dashboard

Consolidated analytics

Client listing and status management

Historical records

Client closing/completion workflows

Custom business-specific calculations

PDF report generation

Excel report generation

Optional password protection for exported reports

Outcome

Instead of relying heavily on manual review, the owner can open the
platform and quickly understand how the business is performing, review
client activity, and access relevant financial reports.

The system was tailored around the company's workflow rather than
forcing the business into a generic finance or accounting application.

Privacy

The company name, client financial information, financial figures,
internal formulas, infrastructure, architecture, and proprietary
business rules are intentionally not disclosed.

04 --- Real-Time Mobile Challenge Platform

Tags: Mobile Platform · Real-Time Systems · Administration

Overview

A custom mobile platform with separate administrative and merchant
capabilities, built around real-time user activity and a managed
challenge-based experience.

The public case study intentionally keeps the product concept and
business model generalized.

Platform Structure

Administration

The administration layer provides tools to manage the platform, oversee
merchant activity, and understand platform-level usage and subscription
activity.

Merchant

Merchants can create and manage challenge-based experiences, including
configurable challenge conditions and rewards.

User

Users interact with the available experiences through the mobile
application and can participate in active sessions and track relevant
activity.

Key Capabilities

Multi-role mobile platform

Administrative management

Merchant management

Challenge configuration

Reward configuration

User-facing activity

Subscription-related platform management

Real-time location-aware functionality

Mobile activity handling

Platform monitoring and management

Engineering Challenges

The project involved significant engineering challenges around:

Reliable live GPS/location handling

Mobile background behavior

Android process and lifecycle constraints

Battery efficiency

Real-time activity reliability

Abuse and manipulation prevention

Client-side and server-side validation

Maintaining a consistent experience across active sessions

These challenges required careful coordination between mobile behavior
and server-side processing while keeping the user experience reliable.

Outcome

We delivered a production mobile platform with multiple roles and
real-time functionality while addressing demanding mobile reliability,
battery, and abuse-prevention requirements.

Privacy

The product name, business concept, challenge mechanics, client
identity, proprietary rules, internal architecture, anti-abuse
implementation, infrastructure, and other sensitive details are
intentionally not disclosed.

05 --- E-commerce & Content Management Platform

Tags: E-commerce · CMS · Business Platform

Overview

A custom e-commerce and content management platform built for a premium
food-export business, combining a customer-facing commerce experience
with a centralized administration system.

The client identity is intentionally omitted from the public portfolio.

The Challenge

The business needed a flexible digital platform that could support
product discovery, e-commerce workflows, customer enquiries, and
frequently changing business content without requiring developers to
manually rebuild pages and information.

Our Approach

We built the public website and administration platform from scratch,
with custom management tools for commerce, enquiries, content, and
business locations.

A reusable page-template approach was also created for different content
requirements, reducing the need for repeated development work when new
pages or content variations were needed.

Key Capabilities

Custom e-commerce website

Product catalogue

Product detail pages

Product and category management

Content management

Quote and enquiry management

Email communication workflows

Interactive branch/location map

Country and branch management

Adjustable branch positions

Configurable map presentation

Reusable page templates

Centralized administration

Custom Content System

The administration platform includes reusable templates for different
page/content requirements.

This allows the business team to use an appropriate predefined structure
rather than requiring a developer to create a new page layout every time
a different presentation is needed.

Outcome

The result is a flexible commerce and content platform that gives the
business greater control over products, enquiries, locations, and
website content while reducing dependence on repeated development work.

Privacy

The client name, internal business information, architecture,
infrastructure, proprietary workflows, and non-public implementation
details are intentionally excluded.
